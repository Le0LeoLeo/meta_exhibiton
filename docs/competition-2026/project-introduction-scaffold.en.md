## Problem and audience

[Describe a specific learning context confirmed by the participating teacher.] Learners may describe activities without making their individual contribution or supporting evidence clear. Generative AI can help with reflection prompts and drafting, but a fluent statement may go beyond the material supplied. This project explores a workflow in which a learner checks evidence and makes the final decision.

## Proposed learning experience

The proposed sequence is: record a real learning experience; choose permitted sources; review what those sources do and do not support; inspect an AI suggestion if the candidate system actually generates one; accept, edit, or reject it with a reason; and submit the work for teacher review. A 2D portfolio is the primary learning surface. 3D display is optional.
